Comfort and elegance for every guest

Hotel Restaurant Elite offers comfortable rooms, thoughtfully arranged for a pleasant and relaxing stay.

Whether you visit us for a holiday, an event or a business trip, our rooms are ready to provide the comfort you need.

Your hotel in Drobeta-Turnu Severin

Our rooms are designed for rest and comfort, in an elegant and welcoming atmosphere. Each room offers generous space, carefully selected furniture and useful amenities for an enjoyable stay. Guests also have access to indoor and outdoor pools, free parking, complimentary Wi-Fi, family rooms, a restaurant, bar, steam sauna, dry sauna and an excellent breakfast included in the room rate.

The hotel is suitable for couples and families, as well as for business travellers. For availability and reservations, please contact us directly.
